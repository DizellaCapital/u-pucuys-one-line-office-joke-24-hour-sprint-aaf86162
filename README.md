One line of office humour, the kind you send a colleague after a meeting that should have been an email — a [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry by [@dizellacapital](https://make.fun/r/qxacafna).

## The joke

> **We held a meeting to discuss why the last meeting should have been an email, and the minutes just say 'see next meeting.'**

A meeting is called to discuss why the last meeting should have been an email — and the minutes of that meeting just defer to the next one. The loop never closes: the record of the meeting about meetings points at yet another meeting. Anyone who has sat through one recognises it instantly.

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the joke to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), validates it is a single line of 1–200 characters with no tabs and sentence-ending punctuation, and verifies the write by reading it back. The entry file is exactly 121 bytes, with no trailing newline.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat "$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt"
```
